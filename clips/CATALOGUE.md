# Reaction clip catalogue

Short reaction clips Anthony downloads (mostly Nollywood and Nigerian skits) to drop into his videos
on exact words. **Catalogued once, reused by every agent.** Read this instead of watching the clips.

- The clips live in his Google Drive, folder **Video Editing / Life/Clips** (shared by link). Never in
  this repo, and never their Drive ids here (the repo is public).
- Local name = the short name below (`projects/<video>/clips/<name>.mp4`); the Drive title is next to it.
- Times are seconds into the clip. A "moment" is 1 to 2.5 s: enough for one reaction, then back to him.
- **Burned in**: text, logos or handles printed on the clip. Crop or cover them when used, so no other
  creator's handle or title shows in his video.
- **Their copyright**: these are other people's films and skits. He has chosen to use them (decision
  2026-11-25); TikTok or YouTube can mute or remove a video for it. Keep each use short (under 3 s).
- **Sound**: "own sound" means the clip's words are worth hearing (dip his voice around it).
  Otherwise use it silent or with a short swoosh.

How to add clips: skill `reaction-clips`.
