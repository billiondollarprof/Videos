# Caption ideas bank, and what each video used

Anthony's rule (2026-11-28): from every caption video he shares, take the ONE best idea, mix it into our own look, and change the
caption a little on every video, so the viewers do not get bored of the same look. Copy ideas, never footage or graphics.

## How to use this file

1. Before a video: read the table of ideas and the log. Pick **our base** (always) plus **one new idea** that this video has not used
   (or the one Anthony asked for). Do not repeat the same recipe as the video before.
2. Switch ideas on in `pipeline/room/kinetic.py` (flags) and say which in the log below.
3. After a video: write the recipe in the log, and what Anthony said about it.

## Our base (every video)
Low on the screen (never over a face), a soft dark scrim, outline, ordinary words small, the key word of each phrase big, no sound on captions.
