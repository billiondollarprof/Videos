# AI picture prompts for a voiceover

Anthony makes these in ChatGPT, portrait 9:16. Rules: no face, no real person, no text, no flag
with writing, nothing that claims a fact. Build them from what he says in commentary, one per
commentary moment. Video 1 used four: a clay cooking pot on a fire (a joke about "cooking"),
a pair of hands clapping in laughter with a phone (face out of frame), a dark street in a
power cut with one lit window, an empty parliament chamber with one microphone.

Template:
> A photo-realistic image of <scene>, <light and mood>, cinematic, shallow depth of field, no people
> (or: face out of frame), no text. Portrait 9:16.

He uploads them to the same Drive folder as ai1, ai2, ai3, ai4, in the order of the prompts.
